\chapter{Disclosure of AI Usage}
\label{app:ai_usage}

Following the TU Delft guidelines on the responsible use of Artificial
Intelligence, this appendix transparently documents how AI tools were employed
throughout the research and writing of this thesis:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Literature Discovery:} Claude and ChatGPT were used for the
  initial discovery of relevant academic literature and sources. All sources
  identified by these AI tools were subsequently verified before inclusion in
  this thesis.

  \item \textbf{Code Development and Debugging:} Claude Code was used as a
  programming assistant to help with writing scripts for
  running evaluations and creating figures, debugging, and understanding codebase
  during the development of this thesis.

  \item \textbf{Text Editing and Rephrasing:} Claude and ChatGPT were used for
  proofreading, grammatical corrections, and rephrasing certain sections of the
  text to improve overall clarity, readability, and flow.

  \item \textbf{LaTeX formatting:} Claude Code assisted with structuring and
  formatting the document, including the layout of tables and figures.

  \item \textbf{Conceptual Learning:} Claude and ChatGPT were used to learn and clarify and
  domain concepts encountered during the research. All
  explanations were cross-checked against the primary literature.

  \item \textbf{Image Creation:} Gemini assisted with the creation of two figures
  describing the model architecture to follow the same style as in the original papers.


\end{itemize}
